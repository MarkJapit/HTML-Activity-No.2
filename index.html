❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is eligible to participate in the competitions?

All bona fide SMCC students currently enrolled are eligible to register and participate in all workshops and competitions.

Is there any registration fee?

None! Registration and participation for all CCIS Week events are 100% free.

Will certificates be issued?

Yes, all participants and competition winners will receive certificates of participation and achievement.
